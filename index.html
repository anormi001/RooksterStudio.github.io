Video Publisher

Video Publisher is a private tool that uploads videos to its owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public. It has no sign-up, no user accounts and no other users; the only person who signs in is the owner of the channel.

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for permission to upload videos to that channel and to post a comment on those uploaded videos. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is accessed and how it is handled.
