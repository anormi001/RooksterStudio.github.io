Privacy Policy

Video Publisher · Last updated: October 12, 2026

What this app is

Video Publisher is a private tool that uploads videos to its owner's own YouTube channel. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

Google user data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests these YouTube permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish videos to the owner's channel.
  • Manage YouTube account content (youtube.force-ssl): to read the owner's channel name, so the owner can confirm the correct channel is connected, and to post a comment on a video the app has just uploaded.

The app does not read the owner's watch history, subscriptions, private videos, other users' data, or any other Google account information.

How the data is used and stored

The permissions above are used only for the actions described. The sign-in credential (an OAuth refresh token) is stored in a file on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any server other than Google's, and it is not stored anywhere else.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used.

Deleting access and data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the locally stored credential file. Videos already uploaded remain on YouTube and are managed in YouTube Studio.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Video Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
